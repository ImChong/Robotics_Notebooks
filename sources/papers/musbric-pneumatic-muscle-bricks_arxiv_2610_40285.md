2. **周报所述问题：** 肌肉骨骼机器人改变肌肉连接位置或肢体结构时，通常需要重新加工执行器和骨架。
3. **周报所述创新点：** 将纤维增强伸长型气动肌肉封装成乐高兼容模块，并建立对应 MuJoCo 模型；通过 15 种腿部构型、肘关节、灵巧手和四足行走平台验证可重构设计。
4. **arXiv 摘要（英文节选）：** Musculoskeletal robots combine compliant actuators with articulated skeletons, but exploring alternative configurations often requires repeated fabrication. We present MUSBRIC, a modular hardware platform and accompanying MuJoCo-based simulator for reconfigurable musculoskeletal robots. LEGO-compatible endcaps turn fiber-reinforced extensile pneumatic artificial muscles into reusable, plug-and-play muscle bricks that connect directly to standard LEGO components.
