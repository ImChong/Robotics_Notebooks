## 核心摘录

1. **策展来源：** [【10.26-10.30前沿论文动态】人形／四足33篇](../blogs/wechat_senlanke_weekly_humanoid_quadruped_2026-10-26_30.md)
2. **周报所述问题：** 外部单目相机观察四足机器人时，腿部对称、自遮挡和关节持续运动会造成关键点匹配歧义。
3. **周报所述创新点：** QuadPose 从视频估计六自由度基座位姿和 12 个关节角，并构建首个外部相机四足姿态基准 QuadSyn；推理速度约 33 FPS。
4. **arXiv 摘要（英文节选）：** Quadruped robots are increasingly deployed in hazardous environments such as disaster response and facility inspection, where external-camera monitoring is essential for safe operation. This setting is challenging because quadrupeds are articulated systems with time-varying leg joints, leg symmetry, and frequent self-occlusions, which make keypoint correspondence ambiguous. Moreover, no public benchmark exists, making quantitative comparison difficult.
