2. **周报所述问题：** 偏好条件策略可能虽然响应风格指令，却同时改变无关行为或损害任务性能。
3. **周报所述创新点：** 建立偏好响应度、覆盖率和效用联合指标，系统比较奖励、优势、损失及 PCGrad 层面的目标聚合；揭示早期聚合响应范围更大，后期聚合控制更精确。
4. **arXiv 摘要（英文节选）：** Modern robotic agents are increasingly expected to support steerable control, where a single policy can adjust its behavioral style at runtime under changing user preferences. Preference-conditioned reinforcement learning provides this interface, but conditioning alone does not ensure that a requested behavior change is strong, does not leak into unrelated behaviors, and does not compromise task performance. Our evaluation combines Preference Reward Responsiveness (PRR) with coverage and utility metrics.
