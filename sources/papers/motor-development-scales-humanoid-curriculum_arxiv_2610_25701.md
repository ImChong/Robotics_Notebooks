## 核心摘录

1. **策展来源：** [【10.19-10.23前沿论文动态】人形／四足39篇](../blogs/wechat_senlanke_weekly_humanoid_quadruped_2026-10-19_23.md)
2. **周报所述问题：** 人形机器人学习从躺卧、翻身到站立和行走的一系列技能时，缺少有科学依据的课程顺序。
3. **周报所述创新点：** 把四种婴幼儿运动发育量表转化为技能依赖图并用于 RL 课程学习；通过失败分析发现缺失的「跪姿到站立」里程碑，并验证补齐节点后可恢复完整技能学习。
4. **arXiv 摘要（英文节选）：** We investigate how humanoid robots can learn a series of motor skills defined by human infant motor development scales, and we evaluate which scales are comprehensive enough to serve as self-contained guides for developing gross motor skills up to standing and walking. We represent each motor development scale as a developmental graph that guides the training of a humanoid robot. We then apply this graph within a reinforcement learning framework so that the robot learns skills following the natural phases of human motor development described by the scale.
