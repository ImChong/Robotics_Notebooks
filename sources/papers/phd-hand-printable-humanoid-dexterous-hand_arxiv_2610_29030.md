## 核心摘录

1. **策展来源：** [【10.19-10.23前沿论文动态】人形／四足39篇](../blogs/wechat_senlanke_weekly_humanoid_quadruped_2026-10-19_23.md)
2. **周报所述问题：** 腱绳灵巧手成本高、维护复杂，而传统连杆手指难以在紧凑尺寸内实现多驱动自由度。
3. **周报所述创新点：** 结合柔性铰链和弹簧钢弹性推杆，减少微型运动部件；可使用普通 3D 打印机制造，并在接近人手尺寸的结构中实现每指三驱动自由度。
4. **arXiv 摘要（英文节选）：** Currently, dexterous humanoid robot hands are widely considered the final hardware bottleneck that hampers generalist robots from physically assisting people at home and work. As conventional tendon-driven designs suffer from high cost, demanding maintenance, poor reliability, and short durability, alternative linkage-driven designs have increasingly gained attention. Despite their potential to avoid the weaknesses of tendon-driven hands, integrating multiple degrees of actuation (DOA) in a single digit with linkage drivetrains is a challenge for design and fabrication.
