## 核心摘录

1. **策展来源：** [【10.19-10.23前沿论文动态】人形／四足39篇](../blogs/wechat_senlanke_weekly_humanoid_quadruped_2026-10-19_23.md)
2. **周报所述问题：** 建筑机器人需要在分离的供料区和施工区之间移动，并准确执行 CAD 定义的砌筑任务。
3. **周报所述创新点：** 将 CAD 砖块位姿直接转换为 Spot 操作任务，以 AprilTag 定位并通过 Autowalk 完成区域间导航；在重复曲面墙体搭建实验中定量测量放置误差，并进行室外楼梯测试。
4. **arXiv 摘要（英文节选）：** Mobile legged robots offer unique advantages for on-site construction, including the ability to traverse unstructured terrain, climb stairs, and operate independently between spatially separated work zones. This paper presents a proof-of-concept system for autonomous brick stacking using a Boston Dynamics Spot quadrupedal robot equipped with a manipulator arm. The system operates between a supply area and an assembly area, using AprilTag fiducials for localization and autowalk missions for navigation.
