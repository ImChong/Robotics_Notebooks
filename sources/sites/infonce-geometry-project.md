## 项目页核心摘录

1. **Core claim：** unimodal InfoNCE → strictly convex Gibbs landscape；symmetric multimodal → negative symmetric divergence → modality gap under heterogeneity。
2. **四步 roadmap：** deterministic limit → unimodal cohesion → multimodal bifurcation → CLIP/COCO validation。
3. **Takeaway：** pairwise alignment alone insufficient for cross-modal marginal structure。
