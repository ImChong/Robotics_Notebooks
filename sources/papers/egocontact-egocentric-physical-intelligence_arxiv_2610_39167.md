## 核心摘录

1. **策展来源：** [【10.26-10.30前沿论文动态】Manipulation 57篇](../blogs/wechat_senlanke_weekly_manipulation_2026-10-26_30.md)
2. **周报所述问题：** 人类第一视角视频被广泛用于机器人预训练，但模型是否真正理解物体应该在哪里接触，缺少直接评测。
3. **周报所述创新点：** 构建包含 22,500 个人工验证交互事件的数据集，提供接触前观测、操作意图和像素级接触位置。将 EgoContact 作为可供性迁移策略的记忆库后，真实机器人抓取成功率从 **16% 提升至 58%**。
4. **arXiv 摘要（英文节选）：** Egocentric video has emerged as one of the most scalable sources of data for learning generalizable manipulation priors. Yet, despite its growing use in pretraining robotic policies, it has been underutilized as a validation tool for physical intelligence, a key component of which is understanding where to make contact with an object to achieve an intended interaction. We present EgoContact, a large-scale egocentric dataset for evaluating the generalizability and precision of learned manipulation priors through future contact-point prediction.
