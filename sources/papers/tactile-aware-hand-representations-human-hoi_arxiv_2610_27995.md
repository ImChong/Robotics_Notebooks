## 核心摘录

1. **策展来源：** [【10.19-10.23前沿论文动态】Manipulation 37篇](../blogs/wechat_senlanke_weekly_manipulation_2026-10-19_23.md)
2. **周报所述问题：** 人类手部操作数据包含丰富的接触行为，但单纯学习手部姿态，难以完整表示手与物体之间的触觉交互。
3. **周报所述创新点：** 将触觉相关信息引入手部表示学习，使模型能够从人手与物体交互数据中学习更适合接触操作的手部特征。
4. **arXiv 摘要（英文节选）：** Tactile representation learning for dexterous manipulation often requires substantial robot-side data collection, limiting its scalability across different hands and sensors. We explore whether large-scale human hand-object interaction (HOI) data can provide a useful prior for robotic tactile learning. To this end, we propose HuTact, a framework that pretrains tactile-aware hand representations from human HOI data without requiring real tactile signals during pretraining.
