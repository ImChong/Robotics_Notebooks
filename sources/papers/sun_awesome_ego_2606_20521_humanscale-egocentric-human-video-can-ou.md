- **Highlights（清单）：** Systematic study comparing egocentric human video and teleoperated real-robot trajectories as pretraining data sources. Models pretrained on egocentric data achieve 24% lower validation loss, and 52.5% and 90% higher success rates on in-distribution and out-of-distribution tasks respectively
