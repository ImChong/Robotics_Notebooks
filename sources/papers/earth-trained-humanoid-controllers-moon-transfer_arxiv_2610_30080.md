2. **周报所述问题：** 现有研究很少同时考虑月球低重力、粗糙地形和可变形月壤，也不清楚地球策略向月球迁移是否对称。
3. **周报所述创新点：** 在刚性地面和三维阻力理论土壤上系统比较地球训练、月球训练、微调及重力随机化策略；发现重力迁移高度不对称，而粗糙月壤加重力随机化具有最佳跨环境鲁棒性。
4. **arXiv 摘要（英文节选）：** The Moon changes gravity, terrain, and soil at once. Learned locomotion has studied reduced gravity on rigid ground and granular terrain mostly at Earth gravity, but not humanoids under both, generalist-controller transfer, or the Earth cost of lunar adaptation. On a simulated Unitree G1, we train 12 policies with one recipe, varying gravity, terrain geometry, and ground contact (rigid or three-dimensional resistive force theory, 3D RFT), and including Earth-to-Moon fine-tuning and gravity randomization.
