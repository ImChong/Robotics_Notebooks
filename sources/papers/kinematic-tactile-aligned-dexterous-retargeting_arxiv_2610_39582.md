## 核心摘录

1. **策展来源：** [【10.26-10.30前沿论文动态】Manipulation 57篇](../blogs/wechat_senlanke_weekly_manipulation_2026-10-26_30.md)
2. **周报所述问题：** 传统人手到机器人灵巧手的重定向主要匹配关节姿态，无法保留人类操作中的接触力信息。
3. **周报所述创新点：** 提出三阶段运动学—触觉联合重定向框架。先对齐手部运动和接触几何，再构建人手接触力旋量支持空间，最后优化机器人指尖接触力并转换为关节位置修正。真实机器人倾倒实验中的轨迹重放成功率达到 **96.7%**。
4. **arXiv 摘要（英文节选）：** Human demonstrations provide a natural and scalable source of data for learning dexterous manipulation policies. However, existing retargeting methods primarily transfer human kinematics, while the tactile information underlying physical interaction is rarely preserved across embodiments. To address this limitation, we present a hierarchical three-stage framework for kinematic-tactile aligned dexterous retargeting.
