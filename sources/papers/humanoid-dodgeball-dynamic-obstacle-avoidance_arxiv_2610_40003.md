2. **周报所述问题：** 面对快速飞来的物体，人形机器人必须在很短时间内完成全身闪避，同时保持平衡和动作自然性。
3. **周报所述创新点：** 使用威胁感知奖励训练反应式 RL 策略，并用多个方向特定的对抗动作先验约束闪避动作；在 EngineAI PM01 上验证跨速度、跨发射距离的前跨和后跳闪避。
4. **arXiv 摘要（英文节选）：** Humanoids have become increasingly agile and capable in real-world environments. To operate safely in dynamic scenarios, they must rapidly evade fast-moving obstacles while maintaining stability and physically feasible whole-body motion. Learning such behaviors remains challenging due to the high-dimensional dynamics of humanoid systems and the need to jointly balance reactive obstacle avoidance, motion naturalness, and control robustness.
