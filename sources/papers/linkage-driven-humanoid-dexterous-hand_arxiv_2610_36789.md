## 核心摘录

1. **策展来源：** [【10.26-10.30前沿论文动态】人形／四足33篇](../blogs/wechat_senlanke_weekly_humanoid_quadruped_2026-10-26_30.md)
2. **周报所述问题：** 现有灵巧手难以同时实现类人关节结构、高负载、机械可靠性和简单可控性。
3. **周报所述创新点：** Torch-Hand 采用全连杆传动，具有 21 自由度，其中拇指具有五自由度和多轴腕掌关节；所有执行器位于手掌内，并通过机械解耦减少非目标关节联动。
4. **arXiv 摘要（英文节选）：** Humanoid dexterous hands are a core component of embodied intelligence, enabling robots to translate high-level intent into rich and adaptive physical interaction. However, existing designs remain constrained by a persistent trade-off among anthropomorphic kinematic complexity, mechanical robustness, load capacity and control simplicity, limiting dexterous manipulation in unstructured environments. Here we present Torch-Hand, a fully articulated humanoid dexterous hand that replicates the joint structure and motion hierarchy of the human hand with 21 degrees of freedom (DOF), comprising a highly articulated five-DOF thumb and four DOF in each of the remaining fingers.
