- **Contribution（清单）：** RoboNet pools robot experience to make visual control transferable. Pretraining improves adaptation with a few hundred target-robot trajectories, but relevant subsets can outperform the broader pool. Its central contribution is a shared dataset evaluated through two distinct control algorithms.
