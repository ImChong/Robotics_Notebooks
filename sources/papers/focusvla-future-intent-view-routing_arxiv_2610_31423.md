## 核心摘录

1. **策展来源：** [【10.19-10.23前沿论文动态】Manipulation 37篇](../blogs/wechat_senlanke_weekly_manipulation_2026-10-19_23.md)
2. **周报所述问题：** 多相机 VLA 在不同操作阶段需要关注不同视角，但统一处理全部相机信息会增加计算开销，也可能引入无关视觉内容。
3. **周报所述创新点：** 将未来操作意图引入视角路由，根据即将执行的动作动态选择视觉信息，使视觉处理与后续操作需求相匹配。论文已被 CoRL 2026 接收。
4. **arXiv 摘要（英文节选）：** Vision-language-action (VLA) models have become an important paradigm for robotic manipulation by unifying visual perception, language understanding, and action generation. Since reliable manipulation requires sufficient observation of objects, end-effectors, and their spatial relationships, multi-view inputs are widely used to provide complementary spatial information. However, our experiments show that existing VLA models do not fully exploit such multi-view complementarity.
