# Compositional CL WM Benchmark（arXiv:2609.22055）

> 来源归档（paper）

- **标题：** Benchmarking World Models for Continual Learning on Compositional Tasks
- **类型：** paper
- **arXiv：** <https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22055>
- **PDF：** <https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.22055>
- **项目页：** https://object814.github.io/Compositional-Continual-Learning/
