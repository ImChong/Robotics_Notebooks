## 核心摘录

1. **策展来源：** [【10.19-10.23前沿论文动态】人形／四足39篇](../blogs/wechat_senlanke_weekly_humanoid_quadruped_2026-10-19_23.md)
2. **周报所述问题：** 月球崎岖、断裂地形需要跳跃越过间隙，但策略必须依赖任务允许的机载传感器，并满足实际执行器功率限制。
3. **周报所述创新点：** 通过多阶段训练得到单一端到端定点跳跃策略；分析控制冻结等极端故障，并在重力卸载硬件实验中验证垂直跳跃及力矩、功率可行性。
4. **arXiv 摘要（英文节选）：** Legged robots can extend planetary exploration to rugged and discontinuous terrain that is inaccessible to conventional rovers. In lunar gravity, jumping further expands this mobility by enabling robots to cross obstacles and gaps that cannot be traversed by walking alone. We present a multistage reinforcement-learning approach that produces a single end-to-end control policy for commanded point-to-point jumping under lunar gravity.
