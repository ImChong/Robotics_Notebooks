2. **周报所述问题：** 普通主动轮腿机器人在狭窄空间内侧移或保持固定朝向移动时受到轮子非完整约束。
3. **周报所述创新点：** 设计安装麦克纳姆轮的轮式足端，并与 EM-Act 腿部执行器组成 OmniQuad；机器人可在不改变机身朝向的情况下完成前后、横向和斜向移动，并保留腿式越障能力。
4. **arXiv 摘要（英文节选）：** Combining wheeled and legged locomotion within a single robotic platform offers the potential to harness the advantages of both systems. The development of such hybrid systems remains an active area of research. While legged robots exhibit remarkable adaptability for traversing uneven and rugged terrains, their relatively low speed limits their practicality in indoor environments.
