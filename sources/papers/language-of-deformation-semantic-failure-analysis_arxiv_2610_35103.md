## 核心摘录

1. **策展来源：** [【10.26-10.30前沿论文动态】Manipulation 57篇](../blogs/wechat_senlanke_weekly_manipulation_2026-10-26_30.md)
2. **周报所述问题：** 可变形物体操作中的失败不仅包括滑移，还可能出现屈曲、塑性变形、局部压力异常和撕裂。传统刚体操作中的失败判断标准难以直接适用。
3. **周报所述创新点：** 提出针对可变形物体的语义失败分析框架，将不同物理失败现象组织为可识别的事件类型，并通过触觉实验研究其可检测性。
4. **arXiv 摘要（英文节选）：** Dexterous manipulation of rigid objects has reached a level of maturity where grasp stability, control, and planning strategies are successfully applied in industry and logistics. In contrast, manipulating deformable objects (cables, plastic pieces, cloth) remains far more challenging, as rigid-object methods do not account for their additional complexity. This paper identifies semantic failure analysis as a key step toward mature deformable-object manipulation and supports this claim with proof-of-concept experiments.
