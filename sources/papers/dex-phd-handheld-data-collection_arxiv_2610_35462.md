## 核心摘录

1. **策展来源：** [【10.26-10.30前沿论文动态】人形／四足33篇](../blogs/wechat_senlanke_weekly_humanoid_quadruped_2026-10-26_30.md)
2. **周报所述问题：** 机器人运动链遥操作能够准确记录状态，但不便携；纯视觉采集便携，却缺少准确的手指状态和接触数据。
3. **周报所述创新点：** 将灵巧机器人手、逐指控制、腕部相机、关节状态和指尖接触传感集成到无系留手持设备；采集的数据可在实体人形机器人上直接开环回放。
4. **arXiv 摘要（英文节选）：** Existing manipulation data collection methods typically offer either accurate robot-state acquisition through kinematic-chain-based teleoperation or convenient, portable operation through vision-based approaches. We present DEX-PHD, a portable handheld device that combines these advantages for dexterous manipulation by integrating a dexterous robot hand, continuous per-finger control, and multimodal logging. The system records proprioceptive information, fingertip contact data, and wrist-camera video, while the operator directly controls the robot hand through joint-level mapping.
