## 核心摘录

1. **策展来源：** [【10.26-10.30前沿论文动态】人形／四足33篇](../blogs/wechat_senlanke_weekly_humanoid_quadruped_2026-10-26_30.md)
2. **周报所述问题：** 四足机器人牵引欠驱动货车时，组合系统动力学复杂，在狭窄空间中可行路径范围很小。
3. **周报所述创新点：** 条件流匹配模型为 MPPI 提供初始轨迹，再由基于学习前向动力学模型的 MPPI 迭代修正；全板载系统可牵引接近机器人自重的 40 kg 负载。
4. **arXiv 摘要（英文节选）：** Legged robots have demonstrated advanced capabilities across a wide range of real-world tasks, yet much of this progress concerns the robot controlling only itself. Once the robot is coupled to an external object -- particularly an underactuated one -- the dynamics of the combined assembly become more complex. Towing a wagon is a common practical scenario, where coupled dynamics make reliable navigation challenging.
