## 核心摘录

1. **策展来源：** [【10.19-10.23前沿论文动态】Manipulation 37篇](../blogs/wechat_senlanke_weekly_manipulation_2026-10-19_23.md)
2. **周报所述问题：** 仅根据视觉预测操作结果，难以完整表达接触丰富任务中的物理交互。机器人策略评估还需要考虑接触状态及其变化。
3. **周报所述创新点：** Physical Canvas 将视觉与触觉共同用于世界建模，研究通过多模态物理预测支持机器人策略评估和策略学习。
4. **arXiv 摘要（英文节选）：** Evaluating and post-training robot policies through physical interaction is costly and difficult to scale, motivating action-conditioned world models as interactive simulators. However, visually plausible predictions can misrepresent contact dynamics, providing unreliable feedback for policy evaluation and learning. We introduce PhysCanvas, a state-consistent visuo-tactile world model for contact-rich robot manipulation.
