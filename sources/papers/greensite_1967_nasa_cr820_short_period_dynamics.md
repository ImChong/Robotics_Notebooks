## 核心摘录

1. **方法论（引言）。** 用小扰动线性化研究自动驾驶仪品质："one adopts the classical method of studying the perturbations from nominal; this results in a linear system for which powerful analytical tools are available."
2. **建模要素（引言）。** "these so-called perturbation equations will be derived taking account of such factors as c.g. (center of gravity) eccentricity, bending, propellant sloshing, and engine inertia."
3. **目录（第 3 节）。** 刚体方程与欧拉角 → 弹性振动方程 → 力与力矩（重力、推力、晃动、发动机惯性、气动）→ 完整扰动方程 → 简化传递函数。符号表区分"control (gimballed) thrust"与"ungimballed thrust"，并定义万向节枢轴摩擦力矩。
4. **系列构成（前言）。** 16 卷依次为 Short Period Dynamics、Trajectory Equations、Linear Systems、Nonlinear Systems、Sensitivity Theory、Stochastic Effects、Attitude Control During Launch、Rendezvous and Docking、Optimization Methods、Man in the Loop、Component Dynamics、Attitude Control in Space、Adaptive Control、Load Relief、Elastic Body Equations、Abort。
