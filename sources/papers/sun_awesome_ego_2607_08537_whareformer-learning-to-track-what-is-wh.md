- **Highlights（清单）：** The first learning-based solution to the 'Out of Sight, Not out of Mind' (OSNOM) task. A transformer-based model with an updatable memory of established tracks and a track assignment module. Trained on 56 videos, achieves SOTA on 260 long test videos from EPIC-KITCHENS-100, IT3DEgo, and HD-EPIC
