## 核心摘录

1. **策展来源：** [【10.26-10.30前沿论文动态】人形／四足33篇](../blogs/wechat_senlanke_weekly_humanoid_quadruped_2026-10-26_30.md)
2. **周报所述问题：** 被动采集的图像难以准确恢复相机外参和物体位置，导致真实场景与仿真场景错位，影响毫米级操作。
3. **周报所述创新点：** 利用主动挥手动作和可微渲染完成手眼标定，再通过主动触碰及触觉反馈细化物体位姿；在 Unitree G1 上完成按钮和滑块等毫米级操作。
4. **arXiv 摘要（英文节选）：** Real-to-Sim-to-Real (Real2Sim2Real) enables structured world representations for planning, interpretability, and safe execution. Most existing approaches rely on passive perception, which creates real2sim gaps due to imperfect observation. Inspired by how humans perceive actively through actions, we propose ActiveAlign, a framework that leverages active perception to align the Real2Sim2Real gap from interactions.
