# De Luca & Panzieri, Learning gravity compensation in robots（IJACSP 1993）

> 来源归档（ingest）

- **标题：** Learning gravity compensation in robots: Rigid arms, elastic joints, flexible links
- **类型：** paper
- **作者：** Alessandro De Luca, Stefano Panzieri（Sapienza University of Rome）
- **来源：** *International Journal of Adaptive Control and Signal Processing* 7:417–433, 1993
- **DOI：** <https://doi.org/10.1002/acs.4480070510>
- **开放 PDF：** <https://www.diag.uniroma1.it/~labrob/pub/papers/IJACSP93.pdf>
- **入库日期：** 2026-08-13
- **一句话说明：** 未知重力/负载时，用关节 PD + 离散更新的常值前馈学习设定点处的精确 $g(q_d)$；证明覆盖刚臂、弹性关节与柔性杆。
