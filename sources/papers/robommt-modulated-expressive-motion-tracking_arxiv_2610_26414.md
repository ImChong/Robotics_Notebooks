4. **arXiv 摘要（英文节选）：** Expressive robot motion can improve naturalness, effectiveness, and perceived likability in human-robot interaction. When deployed on dynamically unstable platforms, however, reactive execution of expressive body motion may bring the robot to its stability limits, especially when expressivity is decoupled from physical control. In this paper, we present RoboMMT (Robot Modulated Motion Tracking), a modular control architecture that couples parameterized expressive motion synthesis, physics-based character control, and reactive adaptation to spatial interaction goals.
