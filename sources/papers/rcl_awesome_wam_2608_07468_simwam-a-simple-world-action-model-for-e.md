- **Contribution（清单）：** We present SimWAM, a simple yet effective WAM that leverages future-video prediction as a training-time supervision signal. Our SimWAM achieves 91.5 PDMS on NAVSIM, surpasses state-of-the-art WAM-based planners with substantially lower latency, and transfers zero-shot to nuScenes.
