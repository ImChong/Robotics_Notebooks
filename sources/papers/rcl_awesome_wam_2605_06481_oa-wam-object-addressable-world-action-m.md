- **Contribution（清单）：** We propose OA-WAM, an Object-Addressable World Action Model for robust robot manipulation. OA-WAM matches strong VLA and WAM baselines on LIBERO (97.8%) and SimplerEnv (79.3%), reaches state-of-the-art performance on the most relevant LIBERO-Plus geometric axes, and remains competitive on the seven-axis aggregate.
