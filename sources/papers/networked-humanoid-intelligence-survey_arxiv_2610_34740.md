2. **周报所述问题：** 人形研究通常将机器人视为独立个体，缺少对多机器人共享信息、任务、基础设施和知识的统一分析。
3. **周报所述创新点：** 按通信、协调、协作和社会智能四个功能梳理网络化人形系统，并从交互架构、交换信息、物理耦合、时间尺度、系统规模和机器人形态六个维度建立分类体系。
4. **arXiv 摘要（英文节选）：** Humanoid robots are moving from laboratory platforms toward connected fleets that share workspaces, infrastructure, tasks, and knowledge with other robots and humans. As deployments grow, the operation and improvement of each humanoid depend on information exchanged across the network. However, existing research has largely studied humanoids as individual systems.
