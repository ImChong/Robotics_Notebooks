## 核心摘录

1. **策展来源：** [【10.19-10.23前沿论文动态】Manipulation 37篇](../blogs/wechat_senlanke_weekly_manipulation_2026-10-19_23.md)
2. **周报所述问题：** 柔性衣物的形状会随接触动作持续变化。相机只能观察部分布料表面，遮挡区域的状态难以直接估计，使双臂操作规划更加复杂。
3. **周报所述创新点：** 将 In-Context Learning 引入柔性衣物世界模型，研究如何利用上下文中的交互信息预测衣物状态变化，并支持部分可观测条件下的双臂操作。
4. **arXiv 摘要（英文节选）：** Robust manipulation of soft objects, such as garments, is a critical skill for service and household robots to adopt, but remains challenging due to its high-dimensional state space, and material-dependent deformations. Humans address this challenge by using mental world models that "imagine" the shape and material properties from partial observations, which enables them to predict how actions affect future states. As such, embedding such world models in robots allows for better generalization in manipulating a wide variety of garments in the real world.
