## 核心摘录

1. **策展来源：** [【10.19-10.23前沿论文动态】人形／四足39篇](../blogs/wechat_senlanke_weekly_humanoid_quadruped_2026-10-19_23.md)
2. **周报所述问题：** 步行和飞行具有不同动力学及控制需求，直接使用统一模型难以兼顾两种模式和推力辅助小跑。
3. **周报所述创新点：** 分别离线求解步行与飞行的控制收缩度量，再通过在线能量型 CLF-QP 统一分配控制；同时研制带四个推进器的四足空地平台并验证推力辅助小跑。
4. **arXiv 摘要（英文节选）：** The integration of dynamic legged locomotion and aerial mobility offers versatility that is deterred by challenges in payload distribution, actuation, and the contrasting requirements of both domains. In this paper, we present the design and operation of a multimodal quadrupedal aerial-legged robot. The presence of legged aerial systems also allows for thruster-assisted legged locomotion, which we make possible with a unified control framework using Control Contraction Metrics (CCM).
