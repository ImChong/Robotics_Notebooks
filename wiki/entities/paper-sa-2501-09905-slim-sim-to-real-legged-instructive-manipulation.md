# SLIM

**SLIM: Sim-to-Real Legged Instructive Manipulation via Long-Horizon Visuomotor Learning** 收录于 [Awesome-Real2Sim2Real](https://github.com/sun254667/Awesome-Real2Sim2Real) **第 003/063** 篇，分组 **21 Reinforcement Learning-based Sim2Real**。本页是 **清单索引**：给出它在清单中的位置与原文入口，方法细节和量化结果请看原文。

## 一句话定义

Presents a low-cost legged mobile manipulation system that solves long-horizon real-world tasks, trained by reinforcement learning purely in simulation. A single policy autonomously solves long-horizon tasks involving search, move to, grasp, transport, and drop into, achieving nearly 80% real-world success
